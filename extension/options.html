Iran-resilience

Distort is engineered to stay online when the national network goes dark. When DPI throttling or full filtering is detected, the extension climbs a multi-protocol fallback ladder to find an open path.

  1. 1
    WireGuard (stealth UDP)Fastest path; first to try.
  2. 2
    WireGuard over TCPWraps UDP in TCP for filtered networks.
  3. 3
    V2Ray VMess + WS-TLSMasquerades as HTTPS WebSocket traffic.
  4. 4
    Trojan (TLS domain-fronting)Hides behind a real CDN hostname.
  5. 5
    Shadowsocks + obfs4Final fallback — slowest but most resistant.
